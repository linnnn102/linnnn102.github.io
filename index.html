RaGuard ↗

An agentic vulnerability detection copilot for Python web apps, running entirely on local models.

  • An LLM orchestrator routes specialist small models through a tool-calling loop, with routing driven by one versioned YAML.
  • Every finding is grounded with retrieval over a 994-chunk CWE/CVE knowledge base.
  • A 1.7B specialist, fine-tuned with QLoRA on 820 examples distilled from 11.8K logged teacher calls, then quantized to GGUF for local serving.
  • A finding is only marked confirmed after ffuf reproduces it against a live containerized target. The specialist kept 80% of the 8B baseline's F1 at 2× throughput, at zero inference cost.

JdAtlas ↗

A 3D map of the AI/ML engineering job market, rebuilt every day from ~8,000 postings. Terms are positioned by co-occurrence, and a sanity gate fails the run rather than publishing a broken map.

IntelligentBistro ↗

A mobile ordering app where you can just tell the assistant what you want. Four constrained Gemini tools with Zod-validated calls keep malformed requests out of the cart.
